🃏 How to Play

Goal: Finish with the most banked chips.

Each player gets 3, 5, or 10 turns, selected before the game. Every turn starts with 100 chips.

Guess whether the next card will be higher or lower (2 low, Ace high). Each correct guess doubles your unbanked pot. Matching ranks are a push; a wrong guess loses the unbanked pot.

Bank Chips to secure your winnings, or press your luck. Double or Nothing risks the pot on one more guess: win to double and bank automatically, or lose it all.

🏆 When does it end? Automatically after every player finishes their selected turns. The player with the most banked chips wins. Tied top scores share the win.
