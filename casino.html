🎟️ Players at the Table

Up to eight players. Each player starts a turn with 100 chips. Guess higher or lower, then bank your winnings or press your luck. Correct guesses double your turn's pot. A wrong guess loses the unbanked pot. Equal ranks are a push: no win or loss.

VS

Ranks run 2 through Ace (Ace high). A correct Double or Nothing guess doubles the pot and banks it immediately; a wrong one loses the pot. After a result, the new card becomes the starting card for the next guess.
